\title{The conjectured generating function of OEIS A121138,\\ \large from the recurrence the entry

\begin{abstract}
OEIS A121138 states a linear recurrence as a fact and carries a rational generating function as an
empirical conjecture. The conjecture follows from the recurrence by polynomial algebra alone:
the recurrence fixes the denominator, the published terms fix the numerator exactly, and the
generating function is then determined as a rational function rather than checked on finitely
many coefficients. No model of the sequence and no reading of its name is required.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\small 2020 Mathematics Subject Classification. 11B37, 05A15, 30B10.\normalsize

\section{What the entry says}

The first terms are $1, 1, 3, 10, 46, 192, 840, 3584,\dots$, with offset $1$. The entry states, as a fact,

\begin{quote}\raggedright a(n) = 8*a(n-1)-12*a(n-2)-32*a(n-3)+64*a(n-4) for n>7.\end{quote}

\noindent and separately records the conjecture

\begin{quote}\raggedright Empirical G.f.: -3/128 + x\^{}3/4 + x\^{}2/2 + 49*x/64 - (296*x\^{}3 - 140*x\^{}2 + 6*x + 3)/(128*(2*x-1)*(2*x+1)*(4*x-1)\^{}2). - \_Vaclav Kotesovec\_, Nov 29 2012\end{quote}

The stated line was taken from the entry's factual formulas only. A formula qualified by a
finite range -- "holds at least up to $n=1000$ but is not known to hold in general" -- is not a
fact and is not used, nor is anything inside a conjectural block.

\section{The argument}

Write $b_k=a(1+k)$ and
\[
  D(x)=1-\sum_{i} c_i x^{i}=- 64 x^{4} + 32 x^{3} + 12 x^{2} - 8 x + 1 ,
\]
the polynomial of the stated recurrence, of degree $4$. Let $B(x)=\sum_{k\ge0}b_kx^k$.

The recurrence says $b_k=\sum_i c_ib_{k-i}$ for every $k\ge 7$. The coefficient of $x^k$ in
$D(x)B(x)$ is exactly $b_k-\sum_i c_ib_{k-i}$, so it vanishes for every $k\ge 7$ and
\[
  D(x)B(x)=P(x)=\sum_{k<7}\Bigl(b_k-\sum_i c_ib_{k-i}\Bigr)x^k
         =- 16 x^{7} - 24 x^{6} - 30 x^{5} + 30 x^{4} + 7 x^{3} - 7 x^{2} + x ,
\]
a polynomial whose every coefficient is fixed by finitely many published terms. Therefore
\[
  B(x)=\frac{P(x)}{D(x)}=\frac{x^{3}}{4} + \frac{x^{2}}{2} + \frac{49 x}{64} - \frac{3}{128} - \frac{296 x^{3} - 140 x^{2} + 6 x + 3}{\left(2 x + 1\right) \left(4 x - 1\right)^{2} \left(256 x - 128\right)} ,
\]
which is the conjectured expression.

\begin{theorem}
The conjectured generating function of OEIS A121138 is correct, given the recurrence the entry
states.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Immediate from the displayed identity: $D\,B=P$ holds coefficientwise, $D(0)=1\ne0$ so $D$ is
invertible in the ring of formal power series, and $B=P/D$ follows. This is an identity of
rational functions, not a comparison of the $26$ terms the entry publishes -- those
enter only in fixing the $7$ coefficients of $P$.
\end{proof}
